🔒 Privacy & Data Protection Policy

1. Information Collection: We collect personal identity details (full name, email, phone number, operational position) strictly for role-based access assignment, workforce verification, and operational routing.

4. Data Retention: Operational records, audit logs, and attendance rosters are preserved in compliance with Davao del Norte regional management guidelines.
